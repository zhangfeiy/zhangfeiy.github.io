## About Me

I am an **M.S. student in Computer Science** at **The George Washington University** and a research assistant advised by **Prof. Shi Feng**. I study **trustworthy AI**, with a focus on **large language model alignment, model behavior and evaluation, and AI safety**.

My current work examines specification gaming and reward seeking in language models, and how to evaluate evidence grounding in medical question answering.

I am applying to **Ph.D. programs starting in Fall 2027**.
