**Zhangfei Yang**

Under review at **EMNLP 2026**.

This work studies how persona conditioning changes the factual completeness of medical explanations generated by large language models. We evaluate seven frontier and open-weight models across expert, neutral, calm, and anxious personas using an atomic fact coverage framework.
